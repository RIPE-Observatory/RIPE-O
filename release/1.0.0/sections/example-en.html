Example Assessment Trace back to ToC

A compact RIPE-O trace can be read as a chain from assessment question to evidence and provenance. The example below describes an assessment of one work for a question about concerns relating to the timing or absence of study registration. The assessment investigates the question, uses registry and study-design evidence, and records both a human-reviewed and an automated hypothesis. The provenance statements identify the activity and agents associated with the generated outputs. 
@prefix ripe: <https://w3id.org/ripe/ripe-o#> .
@prefix tido: <https://w3id.org/tido#> .
@prefix prov: <http://www.w3.org/ns/prov#> .
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> .
@prefix : <https://example.org/ripe-trace/> .

:assessment1
    a ripe:ResearchIntegrityAssessment ;
    ripe:assesses :work1 ;
    tido:investigates :question2_2 .

:work1
    a ripe:Work .

:question2_2
    a ripe:IntegrityAssessmentQuestion ;
    rdfs:label "Are there concerns relating to the timing or absence of study registration?"@en .

:registryEvidence1
    a ripe:RegistryEvidence ;
    ripe:concerns :work1 ;
    prov:wasGeneratedBy :evidenceAggregation1 .

:studyDesignEvidence1
    a ripe:StudyDesignEvidence ;
    ripe:concerns :work1 ;
    prov:wasGeneratedBy :evidenceAggregation1 .

:evidenceAggregation1
    a ripe:EvidenceAggregation ;
    prov:wasAssociatedWith :automatedAgent1 .

:evaluation1
    a tido:Evaluation ;
    tido:contributesTo :assessment1 ;
    prov:used :registryEvidence1, :studyDesignEvidence1 ;
    prov:wasAssociatedWith :humanReviewer1, :automatedAgent1 .

:humanHypothesis1
    a ripe:IntegrityAssessmentHypothesis ;
    tido:answers :question2_2 ;
    ripe:resultOutcome "yes" ;
    prov:wasGeneratedBy :evaluation1 ;
    prov:wasAttributedTo :humanReviewer1 .

:automatedHypothesis1
    a ripe:IntegrityAssessmentHypothesis ;
    tido:answers :question2_2 ;
    ripe:resultOutcome "yes" ;
    ripe:rationale "retrospective registration" ;
    prov:wasGeneratedBy :evaluation1 ;
    prov:wasAttributedTo :automatedAgent1 .

:humanReviewer1
    a ripe:HumanReviewer .

:automatedAgent1
    a ripe:AutomatedAgent .
