Ontology Design back to ToC

RIPE-O organises research integrity assessment traces around four connected parts: the assessment case, the decision layer, the evidence layer, and the provenance layer. Figure 1 summarises the core classes and relationships of the ontology. Terms in the ripe: namespace are defined by RIPE-O; the other prefixes identify established ontologies reused to avoid remodelling generic provenance, decision-making, bibliographic, authorship, and organisational concepts.

Assessment Case

A ripe:ResearchIntegrityAssessment represents one integrity assessment and specialises tido:Case. The work that is the subject of the assessment is represented as ripe:Work and linked to the assessment with ripe:assesses. A work may be the publication under assessment, a cited work, or another work connected to evidence considered during an assessment.

Decision Layer

The decision layer follows the TIDO pattern. A research integrity assessment investigates one or more ripe:IntegrityAssessmentQuestion instances. Answers to those questions are represented as ripe:IntegrityAssessmentHypothesis instances. RIPE-O uses ripe:resultOutcome to record the outcome and ripe:rationale to record the explanation where available. ripe:OverallIntegrityAssessmentQuestion represents the overall integrity question that synthesises the component questions.

Evidence Layer

Evidence is represented with ripe:IntegrityAssessmentEvidence and its subclasses. RIPE-O distinguishes publication details (ripe:PublicationDetails), registration evidence (ripe:RegistryEvidence), study-design evidence (ripe:StudyDesignEvidence), publication notices (ripe:RetractionNotice, ripe:ExpressionOfConcern, and ripe:CorrectionNotice), and post-publication comments from the research community (ripe:PeerComment). The ripe:concerns property links evidence to the work or author to which that evidence applies.

Provenance Layer

RIPE-O uses PROV-O, through TIDO, to make the assessment trace explicit. Evidence and hypotheses can be generated by activities, activities can use evidence, and agents can be associated with activities or attributed responsibility for generated outputs. ripe:EvidenceAggregation represents the collection of evidence and source records for an assessment. ripe:HumanReviewer and ripe:AutomatedAgent distinguish human-reviewed and software-generated assessment outputs while remaining aligned with PROV-O agent classes.

Reuse of Existing Ontologies

RIPE-O is deliberately lightweight. TIDO provides the decision-trace vocabulary used for cases, questions, hypotheses, evidence, and evaluation activities. PROV-O provides the provenance model for entities, activities, agents, usage, generation, association, and attribution. FaBiO and CiTO support the representation of works, bibliographic information, publication notices, and citation or notice relationships. FOAF and ORG support the representation of people and organisations involved in assessment traces.
