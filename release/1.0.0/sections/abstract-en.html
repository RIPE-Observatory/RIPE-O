Abstract

The Research Integrity Provenance and Evidence Ontology (RIPE-O) describes how research integrity assessments are carried out and how their outcomes are produced. It captures the works being assessed, the questions asked about them, the hypotheses or answers produced, the evidence considered, the activities that collect and evaluate that evidence, and the human or automated agents involved. RIPE-O builds on TIDO for modelling decision traces, PROV-O for provenance, and SPAR ontologies such as FaBiO and CiTO for works, publication notices, and bibliographic relationships. The ontology is intended to make research integrity assessments easier to inspect, reproduce, and reuse.
